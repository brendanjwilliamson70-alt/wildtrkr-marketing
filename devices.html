Supported monitoring options

WildTrkr connects supported monitoring devices to a simple field platform so councils, contractors, biosecurity teams and environmental teams can see trap status, receive activation alerts, organise devices into Programs, assign responsibility, and record field outcomes.

Device and subscription pricing are managed separately to help procurement teams compare deployment options with clear commercial inputs.
