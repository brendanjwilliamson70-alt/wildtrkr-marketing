How Gateway monitoring works

Nearby traps. One connected system.

Each nearby trap uses a small wireless sensor that reports through a central cellular Gateway. Coverage depends on placement, vegetation and terrain; plan for approximately 10 metres in tested field conditions.

The Gateway provides connectivity for nearby sensors; each trap remains individually visible in WildTrkr. Compare standard Gateway pricing and the first-order Starter Pack offer →

Coverage, terrain, trap type, trap spacing and reporting requirements can all affect the right setup. WildTrkr can help assess whether an individual cellular device or a Gateway + sensor system is the best fit for your program.
