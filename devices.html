Supported monitoring options

WildTrkr connects supported monitoring devices to one field platform so conservation, biosecurity, wildlife management and field operations teams can see trap status, receive activation alerts, organise devices into Programs, assign responsibility and record field outcomes.

Device and subscription pricing are managed separately to help procurement teams compare deployment options with clear commercial inputs.

Best for individual traps

Cellular monitoring device

  • One monitoring device per trap
  • Activation alerts when the trap is set
  • Scheduled device health check-ins

  • Field outcome logging and Program records
  • No separate Gateway required

Best for nearby traps & lightweight applications

Gateway + trap sensors

Use small wireless sensors on nearby traps and connect them through a central WildTrkr Gateway.

  • Designed for groups of nearby traps
  • Small, lightweight sensor at each trap
  • Individual trap activation visibility in WildTrkr
  • One Gateway connects the local sensor system
  • Useful where a full monitoring unit is too large or unnecessary
  • Coverage depends on terrain, vegetation and placement

Gateway hardware is $350 + GST. Sensors are $350 + GST per pack of five.

Monitoring is $30 per Gateway per month + GST, including connected sensor monitoring.
