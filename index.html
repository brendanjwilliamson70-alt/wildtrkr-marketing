How It Works

A straightforward six-step workflow helps field teams run practical monitoring programs with clearer visibility and faster response.

Step 1

Set and assign traps

Allocate active devices and ownership to the right operators from day one.

Step 2

Monitor trap status

Use live status updates across your network to see what needs attention.

Step 3

Receive activation alerts

Get clear email notifications when triggered events require field response.

Step 4

Respond in the field

Coordinate tasks with assigned operators to keep response workflows consistent.

Step 5

Record capture outcomes

Log outcomes and notes in one place to support cleaner records and oversight.

Step 6

Export activity reports

Review activity outputs to support council reporting and program decisions.

Platform Features

WildTrkr combines telemetry, operator workflows and reporting in one practical platform for councils and field teams.

Live Map + List view

See network status clearly across locations with simple list and map context.

Status-based workflow

Work from clear status signals to prioritise response and routine checks.

Operator assignment

Assign monitoring responsibility to teams and individuals by deployment area.

Email activation alerts

Route trigger notifications to nominated recipients for faster field action.

Capture outcome logging

Maintain field records with structured outcome entries and inspection notes.

Trial activity reporting

Summarise early-stage and operational activity to support governance reviews.
