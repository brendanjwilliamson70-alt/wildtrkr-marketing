Programs and teams

One team or multiple programs. Keep the work connected.

Bring your devices, people and field activities together around the program they belong to. Start with a small team, or coordinate several programs with clear responsibility and visibility for management.

01

Clear responsibility

Nominate the person responsible for each program and update that responsibility when arrangements change. Keep your team clear on who is looking after the work.

02

Coordinated field work

Manage deployed devices within a program. Bring capture records and field activities together, including shooting, baiting and site treatment where those modules are enabled.

03

Visibility for management

Give field staff a view of the work assigned to them, while managers review program activity, responsibility and recorded outcomes.

A practical way to organise a few traps. A shared view as your teams and programs grow.

Status to action

See what needs attention, and who owns it.

Map + List brings teams, responsible people, Programs and devices together. Managers can see what has triggered, what needs checking, what is active and what is not deployed, while field staff can see the work assigned to them.

Record the response

Complete the field work. Keep the record.

Record captures, false activations and other outcomes as the work is completed. Field staff can enter the details on site, giving managers a connected record to review.

  • Fast field entry: select the outcome, add notes if needed, and save
  • Saved details remain connected to the Program and support reporting

Program reporting

Prove the work without rebuilding the story later.

WildTrkr keeps device events, field activity and outcomes in structured records so managers can review what happened without reconstructing the program from notes, calls and spreadsheets.

  • Program activity and device utilisation
  • Activation, capture and response history
  • Missed heartbeat follow-ups
  • Export the Trial Activity Report as a CSV for review and sharing

Experience in the field

Practical value when it matters.

Recovering a stolen cage

After a deployed cage was stolen, a council used the monitoring device’s GPS location to locate and recover it.

Location visibility helped the team recover valuable equipment.

Responding to deer-trap activations

Monitoring devices on deer cages alerted the team when a trap activated, helping them respond quickly and reduce the time animals spent in the trap.

Earlier awareness supported a prompt field response.
