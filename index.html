WildTrkr is field monitoring software for trap deployment, activation alerts, operator workflows, and capture reporting. Built for practical, reliable operations in Australian field conditions.

How It Works

  1. Set and assign traps
  2. Monitor trap status
  3. Receive activation alerts
  4. Respond in the field
  5. Record capture outcomes
  6. Export activity reports

Features

Live Map + List view

Track deployments spatially and operationally in one workflow.

Device status workflow

Not deployed, Active, Missed check-in, and Triggered states for clear action.

Operator assignment

Allocate responsibility by area, contractor, or operational team.

Email alerts

Receive fast notifications when traps trigger or require attention.

Capture outcome logging

Record field outcomes consistently for audit and performance tracking.

Trial Activity reporting

Export practical reports for review, planning, and program governance.
