Experience in the field

Practical value when it matters.

Trap security

Location monitoring helped recover a stolen cage

The situation: A monitored cage trap disappeared from its deployment location during a field program.

What WildTrkr provided: The monitoring device continued reporting its location after the cage had been moved, giving the field team visibility of where the equipment had gone.

The result: The location information allowed the cage to be traced and recovered rather than written off as lost equipment.

One monitoring device. Trap monitoring and asset visibility.

Faster field response

Knowing when a trap activates changes how teams respond

The situation: Large animal traps can be deployed away from regularly staffed locations, making scheduled physical inspections inefficient and delaying awareness of an activation.

What WildTrkr provided: When a monitored deer trap activated, WildTrkr recorded the event and gave the field team immediate visibility that the trap required attention.

The result: Rather than waiting for the next scheduled inspection, the team could respond based on the actual condition of the trap.

Move from “go and check whether something happened” to “go because WildTrkr has told you something happened.”
