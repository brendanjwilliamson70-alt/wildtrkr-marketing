The field problem

Manual trap checking is expensive, slow and hard to prove.

Field teams manage dispersed trap networks with limited staff, large travel distances and inconsistent visibility. Without a live monitoring workflow, teams waste time, miss urgent activations, and struggle to prove what happened in the field.

Wasted site visits

Teams spend time checking traps that have not changed.

Delayed response

Triggered traps can sit longer than they should before action is taken.

Poor visibility

Managers cannot always see which traps are active, overdue or assigned.

Messy records

Outcomes, response notes and field activity are spread across emails, spreadsheets or paper notes.

How it works

A simple workflow for monitored trapping programs.

1

Set and assign traps

Deploy devices and assign responsibility to operators or teams.

2

Monitor trap status

Use dashboard, Map/List and status colours to see what needs attention.

3

Receive activation alerts

Triggered events are sent to nominated contacts for field response.

4

Respond in the field

Operators can prioritise triggered or overdue traps instead of checking blindly.

5

Record the outcome

Capture results and notes are logged against the device or trap record.

6

Review and report

Managers can review activity and outcomes to support operational reporting.
