Platform

Built for the way field teams actually work

  • Map + List visibility: See active traps by location and status instead of relying on separate spreadsheets or manual checklists.
  • Status-based priorities: Blue, Green, Yellow and Red give everyone the same operating language.
  • Operator assignment: Keep responsibility clear by assigning devices to the people who need to respond.
  • Activation alerts: Triggered devices send alerts to the assigned team so response does not depend on someone checking manually.
  • Capture outcome logging: Record what happened in the field while the event is still fresh.
  • Reporting trail: Keep practical records for review, planning and governance.
